A Canvas companion for students

Your whole semester, in one place.

Gradient sits beside Canvas. It rebuilds your grades, ranks what is due, turns homework into cited study guides with an AI that runs on your laptop, and finds summer research that fits your courses.

Install in Chrome
  • No API keys or passwords
  • Read-only in Canvas
  • AI runs on your laptop
What it does

Four tools that share one view of your courses.

Alex is a sample student with three courses and overlapping deadlines. Each demo plays in the panel on the right, using Alex's coursework.

  1. 01
    Academics

    Know exactly where you stand.

    Every weighted category and drop rule is rebuilt and checked against Canvas. Try what-if scores, plan study hours, and see each syllabus's rules on AI.

  2. 02
    AI Workspace

    Study guides that show their sources.

    Homework Autopilot reads the brief, rubric and course materials, then writes a step-by-step guide with every claim cited. It uses local models, so coursework stays on your machine.

  3. 03
    Automation

    Busywork, done in front of you.

    A visible cursor works through a sandboxed copy of Canvas. Every step is logged, you can stop it at any point, and it never submits anything.

  4. 04
    Research

    Research that fits what you study.

    Interests and skills are read from your own coursework, then matched to real summer programmes and REUs, each with its reasoning and a link to the programme.

Principles

Helpful, without crossing the line.

It stays out of assessments

Gradient switches itself off on quizzes and timed exams. Reference work is never submitted for you.

See it refuse a quiz
Your course rules, quoted

Each syllabus's AI policy is shown word for word, so you know what is allowed before you ask for help.

Private by default

It uses the Canvas session you are already signed in to. Course text only goes to a service on your own machine.

Honest numbers

Grades follow Canvas's own rules. Study plans state their assumptions and show ranges rather than promises.

How it works

Nothing leaves your machine by default.

Install

Bring it into your actual Canvas

  1. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  2. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select gradient/dist.
  3. Open Canvas, sign in, and refresh. Your workspace connects automatically.

This preview uses sample courses. New homework deadlines are set one and two days after the selected demo day. Grades and planning work without AI.
